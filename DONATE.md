# Supporting Upscaler Manager

Entirely optional. **Nothing in the app is gated behind it** — there is no paid
tier, no nagging, and the Support button in Settings is the only place this is
ever mentioned.

The app links here rather than to a donation platform directly, so a channel can
be added, changed or removed without shipping a new release.

## Where to send it

[![Buy Me a Coffee at ko-fi.com](https://storage.ko-fi.com/cdn/kofi6.png?v=6)](https://ko-fi.com/B2W426SLFP)

**[Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/B2W426SLFP)** — takes **no platform fee on tips**, so
apart from the payment processor's own cut it arrives intact. One-off payments,
by card or PayPal.

## Why not PayPal directly

Donations sent straight to a PayPal address carry commercial fees, stay
reversible for months, and expose the recipient's legal name and address to the
payer. Routing the same payment through a tip platform avoids all three, and
Ko-fi accepts PayPal anyway.

## Things worth more than money

- **Bug reports with a log.** The app writes one; attaching it turns "it didn't
  work" into something fixable.
- **Telling us what your hardware actually did.** This project is developed
  against a small number of real machines, so a report from a GPU or distro
  nobody here owns is genuinely valuable.
- **Corrections to the README.** It makes specific technical claims, and some of
  them have been wrong.
